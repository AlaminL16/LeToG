About LeToG

Learn together. Share what you know. Help someone else grow.

Our idea

LeToG is designed as a place where students and learners can discover study notes, share useful learning resources, and learn from one another.

Whether you are studying for an exam, learning a programming skill, or exploring a new subject, the goal is to make useful knowledge easier to find.

Discover

Search notes and explore subjects that match your interests.

Share

Contribute notes and resources that can help other learners.

Grow together

Build a learning community through sharing and collaboration.

Our goal

To make learning resources easier to discover and encourage students to help each other succeed.
